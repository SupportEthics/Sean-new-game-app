# Soulforge Knight — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 4 July 2026_

Soulforge Knight is published by Support Ethics Ltd ("we", "us"). This
policy explains what information the game handles. **Before store
submission this document must be hosted at a public URL** (e.g.
`https://support-ethics.co.uk/soulforge-knight/privacy`) — both stores
require a link.

## The short version

- The game has **no accounts and no sign-in**. We never see your name,
  email address, or contacts.
- Your save (progress, unlocks, settings) is stored **only on your
  device**. Deleting the app deletes it.
- The game shows ads and offers optional purchases. The companies that
  provide those services process limited device data, described below.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows optional rewarded ads and occasional interstitial ads via
Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may process: device
identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, approximate
location derived from it, and ad-interaction data. In the UK/EEA you
will be shown a consent prompt (Google UMP) on first launch and can
choose non-personalized ads. You can reset your advertising ID or limit
ad tracking in your device settings at any time. Google's policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

Purchasing **Remove Ads** permanently disables interstitial ads.

## Purchases (Apple / Google / RevenueCat)

In-app purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play; we
never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to validate purchases
and restore them across reinstalls; it processes anonymized purchase
receipts and a random app-scoped identifier. RevenueCat's policy:
https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

## Children

The game is a fantasy battle game suitable for ages 9+ and is not
directed at children under 13. It contains no chat, no user-generated
content, and no data collection beyond the advertising and purchase
processing described above.

## Your choices & contact

- Decline or withdraw ad consent via the in-app prompt (UK/EEA) or your
  device's ad settings.
- Remove all local data by uninstalling the app.
- Questions or requests: **sean@support-ethics.co.uk**

## Requesting deletion of your data

To request deletion of any data associated with your device (purchase
records held by our payment processor, analytics identifiers, and
advertising identifiers), email **sean@support-ethics.co.uk** with the
subject "Data deletion request" from any address — no account details
are needed because Soulforge Knight has no accounts. We action deletion
requests within 30 days. Game-save data itself is stored only on your
device and is deleted the moment you uninstall the app; we keep no copy.

We will update this policy if the game's data practices change; material
changes will be noted in the app's store listing.
