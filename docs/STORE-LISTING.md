## Full description (both stores)

> **Forge your blade. Face the horde. Rise again.**
>
> Soulforge Knight is a dark-fantasy idle RPG where your knight never
> stops fighting. Merge swords to forge mightier steel, equip a
> four-blade loadout, and carve through wolves, bone warriors, stone
> golems and worse — even while you're away.
>
> ⚔️ **MERGE & FORGE** — combine swords into 40 tiers of legendary steel
> 🛡 **25 KNIGHT SKINS** — from humble squire to the Void Sentinel
> 🐾 **HATCH PETS** — five companions fight at your side; level them with eggs
> 🧚 **RECRUIT THE FAIRY** — a glowing helper who blesses every swing
> 💥 **CAST SKILLS** — Whirlwind, Gold Rush, Time Warp and Battle Fury
> 🔥 **PRESTIGE & RAIDS** — rebirth for Souls, spend them on eternal relics
> 🗓 **DAILY, WEEKLY & MONTHLY QUESTS** — streaks pay bonus gems
> 💰 **OFFLINE EARNINGS** — your knight keeps fighting while you sleep
>
> Free to play. Optional ads reward YOU (never forced to progress), and
> a one-time purchase removes ad breaks forever.
